How to Comment

Anyone may comment on a draft during its Public Review, even if they are not a member of OSTERA. To comment:

  • open an issue in the {{ site.short_title }} repository, or
  • send an email to public-review@ostera.org.

Please include the draft name and the section your comment is about.
